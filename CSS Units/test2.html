Unit Showcase: A Journey Through CSS Units

Experience a short tale styled with different CSS units.

The land of Units Awaits

1. Pixels (px)

Once upon a time, in a pixel-perfect kingdom, the heroes measured every step in pixels. Sir Pixel strides 20px at a time, ensuring precision in every corner of the land.

2. Inches (in) & Centimeters (cm)

In the Queen's royal garden, paths were laid out in inches and centimeters. Each rose bed spanned exactly 4in wide, with regal 1 cm borders of marble.

3. Percentages (%)

The castle walls filled 80% of the great courtyard, leaving 2% padding to roam freely. Such fluid design ensured the courtyard looked grand on every screen size.

em – Relative to Parent

Lady Emila’s scrolls used the em unit. She set her headings to 1.25em and margins to 1em, so when the scribe adjusted the base text size, everything scaled harmoniously.
